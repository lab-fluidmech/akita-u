Faculty of Engineering Science, Akita University, Japan

About

A maple seed spins like a propeller as it falls, descending slowly enough to be carried far by the wind. When many seeds fall at once, do they influence one another through the surrounding air and behave as a group? And if so, under what conditions?

We investigate whether, and why, order and patterns emerge from interactions mediated by fluid flow, using a combination of experiments, numerical simulation and theory. We also apply what we learn to engineering problems such as aircraft wing design.
