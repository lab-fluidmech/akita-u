Earlier research

Flow stability and vortex shedding (Doshisha University)
Linear stability analysis and numerical computation of the onset of vortex shedding and flow patterns behind rows of circular and square cylinders.
e.g. J. Mizushima, T. Akinaga, Fluid Dyn. Res. 32, 179 (2003) doi; T. Akinaga, J. Mizushima, J. Phys. Soc. Jpn. 74, 1366 (2005) doi

Osmotic flow and transport in living tissue (Kansai University)
Flow of water and solutes through membrane pores and the vessel wall (the endothelial surface glycocalyx), with a focus on the effects of electric charge, studied through both continuum fluid mechanics and molecular dynamics.
e.g. M. Sugihara-Seki, T. Akinaga, T. Itano, J. Fluid Mech. 601, 229 (2008) doi; T. Akinaga, M. Sugihara-Seki, T. Itano, J. Phys. Soc. Jpn. 77, 053401 (2008) doi; M. Sugihara-Seki, T. Akinaga, T. Itano, J. Fluid Mech. 664, 174 (2010) doi

Transition in shear flows, and desalination and environmental technology (Aston University)
Bifurcation analysis of the transition to turbulence in plane Couette and Taylor–Couette flows, alongside work on seawater greenhouses, solar desalination and other technologies exploiting water and heat transport.
e.g. T. Itano, T. Akinaga, S. C. Generalis, M. Sugihara-Seki, Phys. Rev. Lett. 111, 184502 (2013) doi; T. Akinaga et al., Desalination 426, 135 (2018) doi
