Prospective students

The Fluid Mechanics Laboratory studies phenomena involving fluid flow through experiments, simulation and theory. Our subjects range from everyday nature, such as maple seeds, to aircraft wings, and each student's project is shaped around their own interests.

We welcome students who

  • are curious about why natural phenomena happen the way they do
  • enjoy building and improving experimental set-ups
  • would like to try programming and numerical computation
  • are interested in energy and sustainability

What you will gain

  • A grounding in fluid mechanics and the ability to apply it to real problems
  • Skills in measurement, image analysis and CFD
  • Experience of explaining your work through conference presentations
